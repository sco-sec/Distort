# Responsible Use

Distort is intended for offline password-candidate generation in authorized security work, including systems you own, systems you have explicit permission to assess, approved penetration tests, authorized red-team engagements, password-policy audits, CTF/lab environments, and defensive security research.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws, contracts, rules of engagement, organizational policy, and authorization boundaries. Do not use Distort to support unauthorized access, credential stuffing, password spraying, credential theft, or other activity outside an approved assessment scope.

Distort generates candidate wordlists only. It does not perform network authentication, collect credentials or hashes, discover targets, or attempt account access.
